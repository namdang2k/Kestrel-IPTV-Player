APP NAME (24 / 30 chars)
STB Portal Player - IPTV

SHORT DESCRIPTION (≤ 80 chars)
Watch your IPTV provider's Stalker portal on Android with a fast TV-style player

Alternatives:
- Stalker portal IPTV player: your channels, favorites and search on your phone
- Connect your STB portal with a MAC address and watch live TV channels smoothly

FULL DESCRIPTION
STB Portal Player turns your Android phone or tablet into a set-top box for IPTV services that use a Stalker middleware portal. Add the portal URL and MAC address from your IPTV provider, and your channel list opens in a clean, fast TV screen built for touch.

★ BUILT FOR STALKER PORTALS
• Emulates a set-top box so your provider's portal recognizes the device
• Profile settings for portal URL, MAC address, serial number, device ID and model
• Multiple profiles: switch between providers or subscriptions in one tap
• Custom user agent for portals with special requirements

★ A TV SCREEN MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Channels grouped by your provider's genres, with logos and channel numbers
• Favorites: star the channels you watch most
• Instant search across large channel lists (tens of thousands of channels)
• Catch-up (archive) channels are marked in the list
• Now-playing channel always highlighted
• Portrait and landscape, with an expanded full-screen video mode

★ SMOOTH, RELIABLE PLAYBACK
• Modern Media3 / ExoPlayer engine with HLS and HTTP live streams
• Automatic retry when a stream stalls or drops, rejoining the live edge
• Automatic portal reconnect when your session expires, keeping the current channel playing
• Adapts buffering to your network and warns you when the connection is too slow for a channel
• Stop, pause and resume at any time

★ STREAM INFO FOR POWER USERS
• Resolution, frame rate, video codec and bitrate
• Buffered time, live latency and measured bandwidth
• Network quality and the last portal problem, with tips to fix it

HOW TO START
1. Get the portal URL and MAC address from your IPTV provider.
2. Open the app, tap Add and enter them in a new profile.
3. Tap the profile to connect and pick a channel.

TIPS
• If the portal cannot be reached or keeps signing you out, try Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1).
• A MAC address should be used on one device at a time; using it on several devices makes the portal sign sessions out.

The app is free and supported by ads.

DISCLAIMER
STB Portal Player is a player only. It does not include, provide, sell or host any TV channels, playlists, portals or subscriptions. You must use your own portal and account from a legitimate IPTV provider, and you are responsible for having the rights to the content you watch. This app is not affiliated with any IPTV provider or set-top box manufacturer.
